# AI Spending icon brief

- Product: open-source macOS menu-bar monitor for AI spending and usage.
- Palette approved by CEO: deep navy `#162336`, bright mint `#57E0B5`; maximum two colors. Anti-aliasing is acceptable.
- Reference: Mirajian's crisp faceted geometric style, not its origami crane subject or logo.
- Construction: distinctive, simple at small sizes, meaningful for spending or monitoring; no borrowed product mark. Explore flat two-color symbols.
- Existing portfolio subjects to avoid: Mirajian's crane, Egopay's tree and coin stacks, and ExactUpscale's nested squares.
- The first gauge was a palette test only, not an approved final logo.
